<h1 align="center">Tux-Force</h1>
<p align="center"><b>The PC games you own or subscribe to, on Linux. Real licenses, no cracks.</b></p>

Tux-Force brings Xbox PC (GDK) games to Linux the legal way: you sign in with your own Microsoft account, licenses come
from Microsoft's licensing service, and only what your account is entitled to will start.

## Why Tux-Force

- **A whole ecosystem, not a single tool.** Runtime, Wine, sign-in and downloads today; a graphical launcher with
  library and store view, automatic prefix setup and a community compatibility list next.
- **AI-assisted development is welcome.** We build with AI tools in the open, with clear rules: a human reviews every
  change, AI help is disclosed, and no Microsoft or decompiled code goes in. See [CONTRIBUTING](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- **Upstream first.** Our core repositories are soft forks. We merge upstream regularly, credit it everywhere and send
  fixes back.

## Status

Farming Simulator 25 plays, including saves, ModHub and Xbox Live. Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024 signs in and reaches
its services. Expect rough edges: this is early software.
